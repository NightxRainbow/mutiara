How Indonesian seafood is controlled

Four stages, each with a responsible unit and a record that can be checked.

  1. Source

    Farms and vessels are registered. Feed, veterinary drugs, water and on-board handling are covered by good practice certification.

  2. Processing

    Processing plants run a HACCP plan for each product and hold a processing eligibility certificate with a defined scope.

  3. Official controls

    Inspectors audit plants on a risk-based schedule. Accredited laboratories test samples. Findings are tracked until closed.

  4. Export

    A health certificate is issued for each consignment, only from a plant whose approval is in force for that market.
