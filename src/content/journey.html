Assurance journey

Six points between the water and the export certificate. Open a point to see the control applied, the unit responsible, the standard behind it and the record that proves it.

Unit names follow the nomenclature in force and will be confirmed with each unit before publication.

  1. Primary production
    Control
    Good aquaculture practice at hatchery and farm: feed, veterinary drugs, withdrawal periods, water source. Vessel registration for capture.
    Responsible
    Guidance: DJPB (farmed), DJPT (capture). Certification control: Pusat Mutu Produksi Primer.
    Basis
    Permen KP 8/2024, Articles 7 and 8. National residue monitoring plan.
    Record kept
    Pond or vessel ID, drug use log, residue screening result.
    Published
    National totals only.

  2. Handling and landing
    Control
    Good handling practice on board and at the landing site: ice, time and temperature, hygiene.
    Responsible
    Guidance: DJPT. Certification control: Pusat Mutu Produksi Primer.
    Basis
    Good fish handling practice certification. Codex CXC 52-2003.
    Record kept
    Vessel of origin, landing time, handling history.
    Published
    Description of the traceability framework.

  3. Processing
    Control
    Receiving checks, a HACCP plan for each product, sanitation procedures, deviation and corrective action records.
    Responsible
    Guidance: DJPDSPKP. Certification control: Pusat Mutu Pascapanen.
    Basis
    Processing eligibility certificate and HACCP certificate. Codex HACCP principles.
    Record kept
    Lot ID, critical limit monitoring, corrective actions, internal audit.
    Published
    Establishment, approval number, product scope, validity, status.

  4. Laboratory testing
    Control
    Risk-based sampling plan. Tests by laboratories whose accreditation covers the method used.
    Responsible
    Laboratories and inspection bodies under Article 10. Harmonisation: Pusat Manajemen Mutu.
    Basis
    Permen KP 8/2024, Article 10. ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation scope.
    Record kept
    Sample code, method, certificate of analysis, link to the lot.
    Published
    Laboratory list with accredited scope.

  5. Storage and transport
    Control
    Cold store and container temperature, logger calibration, alarm review, manual log when a sensor is down.
    Responsible
    The establishment, verified during official audits.
    Basis
    The plant's HACCP plan. Temperature is a critical control point only where the hazard analysis says so.
    Record kept
    Device ID, readings with source label, deviations and the action taken.
    Published
    Reviewed national indicator, once a real data source exists.

  6. Export certification
    Control
    Before a health certificate is issued the system checks that the plant's approval is in force for that product and market.
    Responsible
    The authorised issuing unit of BPPMHKP.
    Basis
    Destination market requirements and the bilateral certificate model.
    Record kept
    Certificate number, lot IDs, seal and container, issuing officer.
    Published
    Certificate check by number or QR code.

Quality in action

Pilot programme in progress. Five to ten processing plants are being assessed through three verification gates. Case studies will be published here after each plant passes gate 3 and agrees to publication. No results are shown until they are verified.
