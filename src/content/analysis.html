Requirements analysis

The reference sites are plain. None of them wins trust with imagery or large claims. They win it by answering one question quickly, showing where the answer came from and when it was last checked. The last online version of MUTIARA failed on that point: the same count differed between pages and expired certificates were shown as active. A foreign auditor who finds one such mismatch stops trusting every other number, so the data rules below matter more than the visual design.
